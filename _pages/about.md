Hi!

I am Yang Luo, a third-year PhD student in Computer Science at National University of Singapore advised by [Yang You](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~youy/), where I also completed my master's studies. I obtained my bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Wuhan University supervised by [Tieyun Qian](https://www.aminer.cn/profile/tieyun-qian/53f47bdfdabfaee4dc89fe30).

My current research mainly focuses on  **video generation**, **large-scale deep learning optimization** and **efficient machine learning**. I'm also eager to discuss intriguing research concepts and open to academic partnerships. If you're interested in collaboration, feel free to reach out via email.
